About Everest Base Camp

The Everest Base Camp trek (5,364 m) is one of the most famous trekking adventures in the world, taking you deep into the heart of the Himalayas in Nepal. The journey winds through picturesque Sherpa villages, ancient monasteries, and spectacular mountain landscapes, finally reaching the foot of Mount Everest, the highest peak on Earth. With a well-designed acclimatization program and breathtaking natural scenery, this trek offers the perfect blend of adventure, culture, and natural beauty

Iconic Highlights

Kala Patthar (5,644 M)

The highest viewpoint on the trek, offering a spectacular sunrise panorama as the first light hits the face of Mount Everest and the surrounding Himalayan giants.

Tengboche Monastery

The spiritual heart of the Khumbu region, set against the dramatic backdrop of Ama Dablam, where trekkers can witness the daily rituals of Buddhist monks.

Tenzing-Hillary Airport (Lukla)

The thrill of landing at one of the most challenging and unique airports in the world, with a runway perched on the edge of a mountain cliff.

Traditional Sherpa Villages

Walk through authentic Sherpa villages, cross high suspension bridges draped in Buddhist prayer flags, and pass sacred Mani walls along the trail.

Trail Route & Characteristics

  • The journey begins with a spectacular flight from Kathmandu (or Ramechhap) to Lukla.
  • The trek starts in Lukla and follows the Dudh Koshi valley, passing through Namche Bazaar, the trading hub of the Khumbu region.
  • The ascent continues gradually through Tengboche, Dingboche, and Lobuche to allow for safe acclimatization.
  • Reach the main goal of the trek, Everest Base Camp, followed by an early-morning climb to Kala Patthar before descending back to Lukla.

Best Time to Trek

Autumn (September–November), best in October: Crystal-clear skies after the monsoon, crisp air, and the best mountain views. This is the ideal time to go, with stable weather conditions.
Daytime temperatures are 10–15°C, while nights at high altitude can drop to -5 to -10°C.

Spring (March–May), best in April: Wildflowers and rhododendrons bloom along the lower trails, the days get longer, and the weather is friendly for trekkers.
Daytime temperatures are 12–18°C, with nights around -5°C.

Winter (December–February): Very cold and snowy, but the trails are much quieter and the daytime skies are a clear, crisp blue.
Daytime temperatures are 3–8°C, with nights dropping sharply to -15°C or lower.

Monsoon (June–August): Heavy rainfall, clouds that hide the mountain views, and slippery trails that are prone to landslides.

Trip Itinerary

Day 1

Arrive in Kathmandu
  • Altitude: 1,400 m
  • Welcome to Nepal! Upon arrival at Tribhuvan International Airport in Kathmandu, the Arga Adventure team will greet you and transfer you to your hotel. After check-in, you can rest or take a relaxed stroll around the hotel area. In the late afternoon or evening, there will be a short briefing on the trek and the program ahead
  • Overnight: Kathmandu

Day 2

Fly to Lukla, Trek to Phakding
  • Lukla altitude: 2,840 m | Phakding: 2,610 m | Trek duration: 3 hours
  • In the morning, you will head to Kathmandu Domestic Airport for your flight to Lukla. Enjoy spectacular aerial views of the Himalayas on the way to Tenzing-Hillary Airport, which sits at 2,860 m. Once you land in Lukla, the trek to Phakding begins. The trail passes through Sherpa settlements, pine forests, and several suspension bridges with beautiful mountain scenery. After about 3–4 hours of trekking, you will arrive in Phakding to rest and spend the night
  • Overnight: Phakding
  • Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner

Day 3

Phakding - Namche Bazaar
  • Altitude: 3,440 m | Trek duration: 5 - 6 hours
  • The journey continues to Namche Bazaar, known as the gateway to the Everest region. The trail gradually begins to climb, crossing several suspension bridges and rhododendron forests. Along the way, you will catch your first glimpses of Mount Everest and other Himalayan peaks. After about 5–6 hours of trekking, covering roughly 10 km, you will arrive in Namche Bazaar at 3,440 m. Soak up the unique atmosphere of this mountain town while you rest and get ready for the next leg of the journey
  • Overnight: Namche Bazaar
  • Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner

Day 4

Acclimatization in Namche Bazaar
  • Altitude: 3,440 m
  • Today is dedicated to acclimatization, giving your body time to adjust to the altitude. You can join a light hike up to the Everest View Hotel at around 3,880 m, which offers panoramic views of Mount Everest, Lhotse, and other Himalayan peaks. Alternatively, you can relax in Namche Bazaar or visit the local museum to learn more about Sherpa history and culture
  • Overnight: Namche Bazaar
  • Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner

Day 5

Namche Bazaar - Tengboche
  • Altitude: 3,860 m | Trek duration: 5 - 6 hours
  • From Namche Bazaar, the trek continues to Tengboche. The trail winds through pine and rhododendron forests with spectacular views of Mount Everest, Lhotse, and Ama Dablam. Once you arrive in Tengboche, you can visit Tengboche Monastery, one of the most important spiritual sites in the Everest region. Today's trek takes about 5–6 hours and covers roughly 10 km
  • Overnight: Tengboche
  • Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner

Day 6

Tengboche - Dingboche
  • Altitude: 4,410 m | Trek duration: 5 hours
  • The journey continues to Dingboche, located at 4,410 m in the Imja Valley. As you gain altitude, the landscape becomes more open and barren, with sweeping views of the surrounding Himalayan peaks. After about 5–6 hours of trekking, covering roughly 12 km, you will arrive in Dingboche to rest and spend the night
  • Overnight: Dingboche
  • Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner

Day 7

2nd Acclimatization Day in Dingboche
  • Altitude: 4,410 m
  • The second acclimatization day is spent in Dingboche to help your body adapt to the increasingly extreme altitude. You can take a light hike up the ridge near Dingboche to around 5,000 m. From here, you can enjoy spectacular views of Makalu, Lhotse, and many other Himalayan peaks. If you prefer not to hike, you can simply relax and enjoy the peaceful atmosphere of Dingboche
  • Overnight: Dingboche
  • Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner

Day 8

Dingboche - Lobuche
  • Altitude: 4,940 m | Trek duration: 4 - 5 hours
  • Continue on to Lobuche. The trail becomes more challenging, with rocky, exposed terrain. You will pass through glacial moraine with dramatic views of the Himalayas, including Pumori. After about 5–6 hours of trekking, you will reach Lobuche at 4,940 m. At this altitude, the air feels noticeably thinner and your physical fitness is truly put to the test
  • Overnight: Lobuche
  • Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner

Day 9

Lobuche - Everest BC - Gorakshep
  • EBC altitude: 5,364 m | Gorakshep: 5,164 m | Trek duration: 7 - 8 hours
  • The highlight of the adventure! The day starts with a trek from Lobuche to Gorakshep at 5,164 m, which takes about 3–4 hours. From Gorakshep, you continue to Everest Base Camp (5,364 m). The rugged, challenging terrain takes you across glacial moraine until you finally arrive at one of the most iconic trekking destinations in the world: Everest Base Camp. Savor the special moment of standing at the foot of Mount Everest before heading back to Gorakshep for the night
  • Overnight: Gorakshep
  • Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner

Day 10

Kala Patthar - Pheriche
  • Kala Patthar altitude: 5,644 m | Pheriche: 4,371 m | Duration: 6 - 7 hours
  • The day starts early with a climb to Kala Patthar, one of the best viewpoints for admiring Mount Everest. From an elevation of 5,545 m, you will take in an incredible Himalayan panorama and watch the sunrise over Mount Everest. After soaking in the views, you descend along the trail to Pheriche, where you will spend the night. The total trek today takes about 7–8 hours and covers roughly 14 km, including both the ascent and the descent with significant changes in altitude
  • Overnight: Pheriche
  • Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner

Day 11

Pheriche - Namche Bazaar
  • Altitude: 3,440 m | Trek duration: 6 hours
  • Today you head back to Namche Bazaar. The trail is mostly downhill, passing through valleys and forests. As you lose altitude, the air gets warmer. Enjoy a more relaxed pace as you look back on the incredible experiences you've had exploring the Everest region
  • Overnight: Namche Bazaar
  • Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner

Day 12

Namche Bazaar - Lukla
  • Altitude: 2,840 m | Trek duration: 6 - 7 hours
  • The final day of trekking takes you back to Lukla. The trail passes through lovely mountain villages, dense forests, and the suspension bridges you crossed earlier. After about 6–7 hours of trekking, covering roughly 16 km, you will arrive in Lukla. Take some time to rest and reflect on the amazing journey you have just completed
  • Overnight: Lukla
  • Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner

Day 13

Lukla - Kathmandu
  • Altitude: 1,400 m
  • Fly back from Lukla to Kathmandu. After arriving at your hotel, use the time to rest or shop for Nepali souvenirs in Thamel. In the evening, the trip wraps up with a farewell dinner at a traditional Nepali restaurant. Enjoy authentic Nepali cuisine as you celebrate completing the trek and share stories from your adventure in the Everest region
  • Overnight: Kathmandu
  • Meals: Breakfast & Dinner

Day 14

Airport Transfer & Farewell
  • After breakfast, you will be driven to Tribhuvan International Airport for your flight home. A legendary adventure comes to an end!
  • Meals: Breakfast

A Note on Meals

During the trek, you can order from the lodge menu each day. However, for your health and safety, we do not recommend (and do not cover) meat dishes beyond Namche Bazaar. At higher altitudes, fresh meat becomes increasingly scarce. We therefore strongly recommend choosing vegetarian options, which are generally fresher and safer to eat on the trek

A Note on Flights

Direct Kathmandu–Lukla flights are usually more available outside the main trekking seasons. During peak periods, especially spring and autumn, most flights may be rerouted via Ramechhap due to air traffic congestion in Kathmandu. Flight schedules and routes may change at any time, depending on airline schedules, weather conditions, and flight operations

TREKKING GEAR CHECKLIST

Base Layers & Clothing

  • Base layer / thermal underwear (top & bottom)
  • Quick-dry trekking T-shirts (3–4)
  • Fleece jacket or warm wool sweater
  • Down jacket (can be rented in Kathmandu)
  • Windproof/waterproof jacket & pants
  • Trekking pants (2 pairs)

Head & Hands

  • Sun hat with a brim
  • Warm beanie that covers the ears
  • Buff / neck gaiter for dust and wind protection
  • Thick layered gloves (inner liners + insulated outer gloves)

Footwear

  • Waterproof, high-cut trekking boots that are already broken in
  • Sandals or casual shoes for relaxing at the lodge
  • Thick mountain socks (4–5 pairs)
  • Gaiters to keep out snow and gravel (optional)
  • Lightweight microspikes for snow (a must in winter/snowy conditions)

Field Equipment

  • Daypack (30–40 liters)
  • Trekking poles
  • Headlamp with spare batteries
  • Water bottles (2 liters total capacity) + water purification tablets / water filter
  • UV-protection sunglasses

Personal & Health

  • Sunscreen (SPF 50+) & lip balm with sun protection
  • Wet wipes, hand sanitizer, and a small quick-dry towel
  • Complete personal medications (including altitude sickness / AMS prevention medication as prescribed by your doctor)

Electronics

  • High-capacity power bank (10,000 – 20,000 mAh)
  • Camera / smartphone with charger
  • Universal plug adapter

Documents & Money

  • Valid passport & copies of your trekking permit documents
  • International travel insurance policy (covering extreme activities & emergency medical evacuation)
  • Enough Nepalese rupees in cash (there are no ATMs on the upper part of the trail)

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does the Everest Base Camp trek cost?

The package price depends on the number of travelers: the more people in your group, the lower the price per person. The price table on this page shows the rate per person in Indonesian Rupiah (IDR). International flights to Kathmandu are not included.

Do I need a visa for Nepal?

Most travelers can get a Nepal tourist visa on arrival at Kathmandu airport: 15 days USD 30, 30 days USD 50 and 90 days USD 125. Make sure your passport is valid for at least 6 months, and check the latest entry requirements for your nationality before you fly. This trip takes 14 days, so the 30-day visa is the safer choice in case of flight delays.

Is the Everest Base Camp trek suitable for beginners?

The trek is rated challenging. You walk for several days in a row, covering around 130 km in total, and sleep at high altitude, with Everest Base Camp at 5,364 m (17,598 ft) and Kala Patthar at 5,644 m (18,517 ft). The itinerary includes two acclimatization days, in Namche Bazaar and Dingboche, but good fitness and some training beforehand will make the trip much more enjoyable. We recommend speaking to your doctor about altitude sickness (AMS) and any preventive medication.

When is the best time to trek to Everest Base Camp?

October (autumn) and April (spring) are the best months, with stable weather and clear mountain views. December to February is very cold and snowy, but the trails are much quieter. June to August is the monsoon season: trails are slippery and prone to landslides, and clouds often hide the mountains.

What about vegetarian and other dietary needs on the trek?

During the trek you order from the lodge menu each day. We do not recommend eating meat beyond Namche Bazaar because fresh meat is hard to get at altitude; vegetarian dishes are fresher and safer. If you have halal or other dietary requirements, please contact us before the trip.

Is insurance included?

No. Travel insurance is mandatory but not included in the package. Your policy must cover high-altitude trekking and emergency helicopter evacuation above 5,000 meters.

What happens if my flight to Lukla is delayed?

Flights to Lukla depend heavily on the weather, so delays and schedule changes can happen. During the peak spring and autumn seasons, most flights may also be rerouted via Ramechhap. Our team will rearrange your schedule as needed, but extra costs caused by delays due to extreme weather, such as additional nights in Kathmandu or Lukla, are not included in the package. We recommend keeping a few spare days before your international flight home.
