About Mount Merbabu

Mount Merbabu (3,145 MASL), located in Central Java Province (spanning Boyolali, Magelang, and Semarang Regencies), is a stratovolcano renowned for its vast savannah grasslands, rich flora and fauna, and breathtaking mountain panoramas.

Iconic Highlights

Vast Savannah Meadows

Expansive green grassland rolling along the mountain ridges, offering an iconic open-air landscape that is truly a feast for the eyes.

Kenteng Songo & Triangulation Peaks

The highest summits of Mount Merbabu, presenting spectacular panoramic views above a sea of clouds alongside magnificent neighboring volcanoes like Merapi, Sumbing, and Sindoro.

Hiking Routes & Characteristics

  • Via Selo (Boyolali): The most popular and favorite route among hikers featuring well-established post facilities, stunning savannah views, and a magnificent, up-close backdrop of Mount Merapi.
  • Via Suwanting (Magelang): A challenging trail renowned for its consistent steep climbs through dense forests before opening up into a highly aesthetic upper savannah area.
  • Via Wekas (Magelang): A reliable route favored by hikers due to abundant water sources along the journey, featuring quite steep terrain with a relatively efficient trekking duration.
  • Via Thekelan (Semarang): A legendary and historic route offering an adventurous journey through dense jungles, local villages, and scenic rocky tracks with rich natural and historical ambiance.
  • Via Cuntel (Semarang): An alternative northern route from Kopeng offering varied terrains crossing plantations, pine hills, and passing iconic landmarks like Watu Gubuk and Puncak Pemancar.

Best Time to Climb

May – September (Peak Dry Season): The best time to hike due to clear skies, minimal fog, dry and slip-free trails, and a high chance of witnessing a flawless sunrise.
