FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions by International Hikers

Get quick answers regarding our private trips and hiking packages in Indonesia.

How do I book a hiking package as an international traveler?

The booking process is very simple. Just contact our team directly via WhatsApp to discuss your destination, preferred dates, and group size. We will handle all details, including official climbing permits (SIMAKSI).

Are airport transfer and local transportation included?

Yes! For private trips, we provide complete logistics services, including private airport transfers (from major airports like Surabaya, Yogyakarta, or Jakarta) directly to the trailhead.

What equipment do I need to bring on my own?

Participants are advised to bring personal cold-weather clothing, comfortable hiking boots, personal medications, and a daypack. Primary camping gear such as tents, cooking sets, and meals during the climb is fully provided by our professional crew.

Is Arga Adventure suitable for beginner international hikers?

Absolutely! We offer various beginner-friendly mountain options like Mount Prau, as well as major volcanoes like Rinjani and Semeru, accompanied by experienced local mountain guides who prioritize your safety at all times.
